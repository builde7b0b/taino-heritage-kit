# Contributing to Taíno Heritage Kit

Thank you! Contributions from members of the community are especially valued.

## Icon guidelines

- 24×24 viewBox, 2px padding, `stroke="currentColor"`, `stroke-width="1.75"`, round caps & joins, `fill="none"` (small dots may be filled `currentColor`).
- Name in lowercase kebab-case using the culture's own words where natural (e.g. `sol-petroglifo`).
- Add the icon's meaning to `icons/icons.json` and `CULTURAL-GUIDE.md`.
- Keep it recognizable at 16px.

## Patterns

- Seamless SVG tile, colors only from `tokens/colors.json`.
- Add a 600×400 PNG preview to `patterns/previews/` and a class to `patterns/patterns.css`.

## Imagery

- Only add images you have the right to license under CC BY 4.0.
- Avoid stereotypes, and represent a range of skin tones, ages, body types and regions.
- Disclose AI-generated imagery in the PR.

## Process

1. Fork → branch → commit → pull request.
2. Describe the cultural context of new symbols, with sources.
3. A maintainer (ideally from the community) reviews for accuracy and respect.

By contributing you agree your work is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
